OLA · THE STORY BEHIND THE WAVE

Wave26

Wave26 is the music side of OLA — the place where songs become worlds.

It started with songs made for moments: weddings, trips, inside jokes, late nights, questions, people we love, and ideas that were probably supposed to stay ideas. Some turned into albums. Some became entire projects. Others are one-offs, experiments, show pieces, alternate versions, or songs that only make complete sense if you were there.

Wave26 isn't meant to be one perfectly organized discography. It's the record of all of it — La Ola de Amor, We See Yes, To AI or Not, Love Lore, Ground Control, and everything floating between them.

If it made us laugh, dance, remember something, or immediately say “wait, play that again,” it found a home here. It’s never that serious. Welcome to Wave26.
