From a thought to a song

A feeling becomes a phrase. The phrase becomes a draft, then another version. Lyrics, AI-assisted music experiments, artwork, and visualizers give each song a world to live in. Listening, choosing, and revising are part of the work.

The published YouTube collections are the listening shelf. The artwork here comes from the same creative process, kept alongside the songs rather than left in a folder.
