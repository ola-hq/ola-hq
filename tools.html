AI-to-AI Handoff

Paste this prompt into the existing AI project conversation, then use the resulting handoff to continue elsewhere.

Create a concise AI-to-AI handoff for this project/thread.

This is NOT written for me. Write it for another ChatGPT/Work/Codex instance that needs to continue immediately with no loss of context.

Include:

1. CURRENT GOAL
What we are ultimately trying to accomplish.

2. CURRENT STATE
What has already been completed and what exists right now.

3. LAST 3–5 IMPORTANT MOVES
Summarize the most recent meaningful actions, iterations, discoveries, decisions, or changes — especially anything another AI could accidentally redo or misunderstand.

4. IMPORTANT DECISIONS / CONSTRAINTS
Anything I explicitly approved, rejected, changed, told you not to touch, naming conventions, source-of-truth rules, folder/file locations, quality standards, etc.

5. OPEN ITEMS
What is unfinished, uncertain, blocked, or still needs inspection.

6. NEXT ACTION
Exactly what the next AI should do first, then what should follow.

7. CONTINUATION INSTRUCTION
Tell the next AI to continue the existing work rather than restart, redesign, broadly reorganize, or re-ask questions that have already been answered.

Be dense and specific. Preserve exact names, file/folder paths, filenames, URLs, IDs, numbers, dates, and terminology when relevant.

Do not explain things for a human beginner. Optimize this entirely for AI-to-AI continuity.
