A SMALL DAILY SYSTEM

Intent → practice → evidence.

01

Choose the intent.

What is today really about? Pick one useful move and give it your attention.

02

Make room for practice.

Move your body. Give a creative block ten or fifteen minutes. Reset one small corner of your space.

03

Leave a little evidence.

Keep a win, a capture or a sentence about how it went. Use it to begin again tomorrow.
