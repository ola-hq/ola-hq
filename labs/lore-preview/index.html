Preview controls and notes

This is a separate sample, not the live homepage. It keeps no mileage, identity or leaderboard data. The accepted globe and sky art load from OLA assets; internet is required. World doors open existing public pages inside the sample. A return greeting requires a loaded frame, a successful matching page check and time inside that world. If the browser blocks verification, the sample does not invent a greeting.

Ship flybys last two seconds, with randomly spaced starts ten to forty active seconds apart. Hover, focus or touch holds the ship so it can be caught. Hidden tabs and open scenes pause the clock. Reduced Motion gives a still vessel and sky. These controls are for reviewing the study, not proposed landing-page settings.
