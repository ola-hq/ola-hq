How to read these numbers

Visitor = one anonymous browser identity, not necessarily one person. Visit / session = a period of activity that resets after 30 minutes of inactivity. Event = an action, such as a page view, click, or engagement measurement. One visit can create dozens of events.

Active time only counts when supported tracking detects an active, focused page. Referrer tells you which website sent someone, not necessarily which app they used. Unknown means the data was not captured or provided. Attachment clicks are not confirmed successful downloads.

Learn as you go · analytics mini-lessons

How many people?

Look at Visitors for an estimate of browsers, then Visits to see how often they returned. Avoid treating visitor IDs as named individuals.

What content works?

Compare Pages, Visitors, and Clicks. High views with few meaningful clicks may suggest navigation or content that needs improvement.

Where do visitors come from?

Use campaign links such as ?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=ola_launch when sharing links. Referral information can be missing.

How do I test?

Open an OLA HQ page on your phone and computer, move between pages, and refresh the dashboard. New analytics data may take time to appear; do not treat your own test browsing as outside traffic.

Visitors are anonymous browser identifiers, so one person using multiple browsers or an installed app may count more than once. Visits restart after 30 minutes of inactivity. App visitors opened the Festival Guide or OLA HQ app route; installed app visits opened in home-screen mode. Installation events are only reported by supported browsers and cannot measure every iPhone installation. Automated tests are excluded.
