A few things to know

Do I need an account to look around?

The public worlds, galleries, music, and Tools page open directly. The personal workspaces below require authorized sign-in through their own apps.

How do I share a world with someone?

Open the world or page you want to share and copy its address from your browser. They can enter that page directly; they don’t need to start at the homepage.

How do I get back to the main worlds?

Use Waves in the HQ navigation to return to the six main entrances. Deeper Waves takes you to the other six currents.

Does a public page open someone’s private files?

No. A public entrance is a link, not permission to a personal workspace. Jon’s Drive and Browser Spaces keep their own sign-in and access controls.

Looking for the personal workspaces?

Jon’s Drive and Browser Spaces now live at the bottom of the homepage, under Protected Personal Tools.
