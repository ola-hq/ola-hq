AIOS · PORTABLE OPERATOR

Give your AI
a place to remember.

A small continuity system for people who want to talk normally and stop rebuilding context from scratch.

The simplest explanation: AI can be very smart and still be terrible at keeping its own desk clean. Even in one long thread, context gets noisy. In a new thread, you often have to feed the important instructions back in again.

This system gives your AI a tiny home base in your own Google Drive. You talk normally. When something matters, the AI compresses it into a clean current state, project record, backlog item, reference, or handoff. Later, a fresh AI can reload that state instead of making you re-explain your life.

Your live Operator belongs in your Drive. This public page is only the installer.

THEN WHAT?

Talk normally.

Voice notes, work, errands, ideas, random thoughts, screenshots, projects — no special format required.

Say “Do your thing.”

Your AI extracts what mattered, updates the clean state underneath, and gives you a tiny receipt.

Start a fresh chat later.

Tell it to load your Operator from Drive. It reads the system + current state and picks up from there.

Don’t like it?

Stop loading it. Nothing runs by itself merely because the files exist.

THE HOURGLASS

Messy in. Clean state. Useful work out.

Large conversations compress into small durable records. Those records can expand back into plans, writing, decisions, projects, and handoffs without rebuilding the entire context.
