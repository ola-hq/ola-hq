AIOS · PORTABLE OPERATOR

Stop re-teaching
your AI how to work with you.

A small continuity system that lets you talk normally while your AI keeps the useful state underneath.

The dumb problem: AI can be insanely capable and still be terrible at keeping its own shit together. Long threads get noisy. New chats lose the setup. Instructions that felt obvious five minutes ago suddenly need to be explained again.

So instead of expecting the AI to magically remember everything, this gives it a tiny home base in your own Google Drive. You talk normally. The AI keeps the clean version of what matters.

This is not “magic memory.” It is a practical way to work with the way current AI actually behaves.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Built by pushing AI until the failure patterns became obvious.

This came out of hours of experimenting — using AI for real projects, pushing it harder, seeing where continuity broke, noticing the same complaints people have about forgotten context, repeated explanations, giant messy threads, and complicated workflows, then trying to design around those limitations instead of fighting them.

The weird realization was that a lot of the complicated stuff in AI workflow videos does not actually need to be maintained by the human. The AI can maintain the machinery if you start it with the right operating instructions.

That is the whole bet behind this: you should not need to become an AI systems engineer just to make your AI stay organized.

THE HOURGLASS

Messy in. Clean state. Useful work out.

TOP — REAL LIFE
Voice notes · screenshots · random thoughts · projects · plans · tasks · decisions · context
↓
EXTRACT → CONNECT → CURRENT STATE
↓
BOTTOM — USEFUL OUTPUT
Plans · writing · decisions · next actions · project continuity · handoffs · fresh-chat reloads
MESSY IN → CLEAN STATE → USEFUL WORK OUT

The neck is the important part. Instead of saving the whole conversation forever, the AI compresses what matters into a small durable state it can reload later.

WHY IT FEELS AUTOMATIC

1 · Start it correctly.

Give the AI the installer once. It builds the structure and operating instructions in your own Drive.

2 · Talk normally.

No tagging system. No project-management ritual. No prompt-engineering homework before every thought.

3 · Say “Do your thing.”

The AI extracts what mattered, connects it to the right place, updates current state, and gives you a tiny receipt.

4 · Reload later.

A fresh chat reads the clean state and relevant project records instead of making you reconstruct everything.

DON’T LIKE IT?

Stop loading it. Nothing runs in the background merely because the files exist. Your Operator lives in your Drive and can simply be ignored or removed later.
