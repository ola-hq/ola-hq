THE HOURGLASS

How the system actually works

You keep doing what humans are good at: dumping context, talking, correcting, creating, changing your mind. The AI's job is to turn that mess into a trail it can reliably follow.

TOP — RICH, MESSY REALITY
voice notes · screenshots · conversations · files · half-formed ideas · decisions · corrections · history
↓
CURRENT STATE · PROJECT THREADS · SOURCE PATHS
↓
BOTTOM — RICH, SPECIFIC WORK AGAIN
writing · plans · decisions · builds · handoffs · finished outputs · next cycles
1. Start at the top. Capture what the person actually means, not just the last sentence.
2. Funnel down. Compress the useful truth into a small current state instead of leaving everything trapped in the chat.
3. Follow the thread. Start with current state, resolve the canonical project node, then follow role-labeled pointers into the relevant references and source files. Threads, not copies.
4. Preserve reference roles. A style donor, photo donor, fact source, layout reference, or DO NOT INHERIT source are not interchangeable. Then expand from the clean state plus the actual source context.
5. Verify and sign off. The AI checks what it actually changed, returns a compact receipt, updates the durable state, and closes the loop.
6. Repeat from the new truth. The next chat or worker starts from the updated state instead of making you explain everything again.

Preservation rule: evolve, do not bulldoze. Prefer append → refine → supersede. Keep the real project where it lives and maintain the thread to it. Never casually overwrite history or claim an action happened unless it was verified.

PRIVACY + TECHNICAL

What this page does — and does not do

Your typed name stays in this browser. It is used to personalize the prompt.
Your live Operator belongs in your Drive. The public website is only the instruction doorway.
AIOS does not have a separate login. Sign in through the AI product and Google account/Drive connection you intend to use. The Operator never needs your password, one-time code, token, cookie, or recovery code.
Before a write, verify the account. If you switch Google or AI accounts, confirm the connected Drive is the intended one before the Operator changes anything.
On a shared or public device, log out of both. Sign out of the AI product and Google account / disconnect Drive when you are done. Logging out does not delete the Operator; its files simply remain inactive in Drive.
Logging is about work, not surveillance. AIOS keeps meaningful project state, receipts, and verification. It should not create a granular clickstream or store authentication secrets.
Live AIOS requires durable writable storage. In this Portable Operator, that storage is Google Drive. If the AI cannot write the Operator files and read them back, it must give you an install/update packet instead of claiming the loop is live.
Quick Install reads one public plain-text spec. Manual Install lets you inspect and copy that entire spec directly.
No fake installs. The AI is instructed to verify created files and write access before claiming success.
Nothing runs by itself. The Drive files are instructions/state, not a background daemon.
