AIOS · PORTABLE OPERATOR

Give your AI
a way to keep the plot.

Keep the richness. Lose the mess.

AIOS gives your AI a small, reloadable home base so you can talk normally instead of constantly rebuilding context.

EASIEST WAY

Install your Operator

Type your name. Copy one prompt. Let your AI build the rest in your Google Drive.

Then: paste it into ChatGPT or Gemini while connected to the Google Drive you want it to use. After installation, just talk normally.

The name you type is used in your browser to build the prompt. This static page does not submit or store it.

What is this actually doing?

Your AI can be extremely capable and still be bad at continuity. Long threads get noisy. New chats lose the setup. Important context gets buried.

AIOS gives the AI a durable place to keep a small clean version of what matters now, plus deeper project records when needed. A fresh chat can reload that state instead of asking you to reconstruct everything.

The important part: you are not supposed to maintain the system. The AI does.

Why an Hourglass instead of a funnel?

A funnel solves only half the problem. It takes rich context and compresses it — which is useful — but if you stop there, everything can come out bland, dry, and generic.

RICH, MESSY REALITY
↓
CLEAN DURABLE STATE
↓
RICH, SPECIFIC WORK AGAIN

The Hourglass keeps the middle small so the context survives, then lets it expand back out into writing, plans, decisions, creative work, handoffs, or whatever you actually need.

The neck is small. The output is not.

Why does this exist?

It came from using AI hard enough on real projects to keep hitting the same failure patterns: repeated explanations, overloaded threads, forgotten setup, duplicate work, generic answers despite tons of context, and workflow systems that became another job for the human.

The useful realization was simple: a lot of the complicated organization people build around AI can be maintained by the AI itself if it starts with the right operating instructions and a durable place to keep state.

This is the shortcut I wish existed earlier.

Privacy + technical details

The live Operator belongs in your Drive. Nothing runs automatically just because the files exist. If your AI cannot write to Drive, the installer tells it to produce an install packet instead of pretending it succeeded.

This prototype keeps only small anonymous interaction counters in the local browser for testing. Central cross-user analytics are not connected yet, and typed names are not included in telemetry.
