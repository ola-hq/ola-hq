“A price tag doesn't tell you who paid for it”

I Love Boosters puts a crew of professional shoplifters up against a ruthless fashion executive. It’s a setup that immediately starts messing with the usual question of who gets to be called a thief. Stealing from a store is easy to recognize. The systems behind the clothes are a much bigger conversation.

Boots Riley gives that clash an absurd comedy frame, using fashion and the promise of belonging as things to poke at. There’s room in the premise for greed, identity, and the way a brand can sell people an entire version of themselves.

For us, this lands as a decent watch rather than a major favorite. The hook gives it something worth talking about, and the whole idea has a mischievous pull. An okay night at the movies with a useful question underneath it: who decides what something is worth?
