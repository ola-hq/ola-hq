“A change of plans can reveal what the plan was hiding”

A holiday can put a relationship under a strange kind of pressure. Away from the routines that make everything feel settled, even a small interruption can expose a much bigger uncertainty. Erupcja gives that interruption a volcanic form, stranding a couple in Warsaw and pulling an old friendship back into the picture.

Charli xcx’s Bethany reconnects with Nel, played by Lena Góra, while the life she arrived with keeps asking where she’s gone. Pete Ohs builds a romantic drama around the distance between having a plan and actually wanting it. The eruption is the disruption; the more interesting movement is between the people.

For us, it’s an interesting detour rather than an overwhelming one. The scale is small, the premise has a distinctive spark, and the relationship questions give it something to circle around. A modest watch with an unusual point of entry.
