“A slow journey to an ending that finally hits home”

Forgotten Island brings culture, folklore, and friendship into a fantasy about the things that connect us. Its Filipino roots give that world something meaningful to hold onto, and there's a sweetness to the story that makes its emotional moments worth sitting with.

The journey there can feel much longer than it needs to. The movie spends a lot of time moving without becoming especially engaging, and the slower stretches make it hard to stay invested. There's a cute, touching film in here, but the pacing doesn't always help it reach you.

Then the final twenty minutes give it the lift it needed. The emotion starts landing, the story becomes more absorbing, and that sweet idea finally carries some real weight. It's an emotional roller coaster with a slow climb, but the ending leaves a warmer impression than much of the ride getting there.
