Aging is scary enough — imagine splitting yourself in two.

Body horror with brains — The Substance takes one of the most universal fears (getting older) and drags it to the surface in grotesque, stylish fashion. It’s not just gore for the sake of shock, it’s a cinematic war between self-image and a society obsessed with youth.

For us, it was tense, uncomfortable, and at times really hard to watch. The movie builds suffocating dread scene by scene, and the commentary hits close to home. That said, the ending dragged on way too long and killed some of the momentum. Still, up until that stretch, it was sharp, layered, and disturbingly entertaining.
