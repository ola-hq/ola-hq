“Desire gets complicated when someone else sets the rules”

Pillion takes a quiet guy’s attraction to a mysterious biker and turns it into an unusual look at dominance, control, and the desire to belong. Colin steps into Ray’s world, where the relationship comes with its own rules, and figuring out what he wants becomes part of the experience.

There’s something interesting about that tension. Wanting somebody and being comfortable with the place they give you aren’t necessarily the same thing. The film gives that idea a bizarre, sometimes funny shape, leaving plenty to sit with as attraction becomes a question of who gets to decide how everything works.

The weirdness is part of the appeal. There are moments where the humor makes the dynamic entertaining, even as the control underneath it gives you something more complicated to think about. It’s an odd relationship to follow, and that mix of curiosity, desire, and discomfort keeps it interesting.

Overall, it’s a decent watch with an unusual perspective on what people want from each other. Funny and strange at times, Pillion leaves the strongest impression through the questions its relationship raises.
