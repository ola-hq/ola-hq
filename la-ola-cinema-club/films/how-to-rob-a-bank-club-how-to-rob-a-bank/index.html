Watched November 11, 2026

“A fun heist with the volume turned all the way up.”

How to Rob a Bank feels very millennial and Gen Z in its humor and energy. There are plenty of funny jokes, and the film is clearly aiming for a busy, overstimulating ride. Sometimes that works; sometimes it feels like it is trying a little too hard.

A few plot points feel unnecessary, and the constant activity can become a lot. Still, the overall experience is fun. The excess seems intentional, even when it gets in its own way: a lively heist comedy that we enjoyed more than its messier choices might suggest.

From the Brain

Funny and energetic, but sometimes trying too hard. The overstimulation and a few unnecessary plot detours make the film feel busier than it needs to be.

From the Heart

A fun film with plenty of jokes and chaotic energy. Even when it goes overboard, that loud millennial-and-Gen-Z sense of humor is part of the appeal.

Mixed BagThings we enjoyed alongside things that did not quite come together.
