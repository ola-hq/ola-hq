“A familiar crime story with a refreshing pull”

Crime 101 settles into Los Angeles with a thief, a detective, and an insurance broker whose paths start closing in on one another. It’s a familiar crime-movie setup, but there’s something refreshing about getting caught up in a story where the interest comes from watching people make their next move.

The appeal is in following how those lives connect. There’s enough going on to stay curious about where it’s headed, and the movie gives its situation room to unfold. It makes for an entertaining watch without needing every moment to feel like the biggest thing you’ve ever seen.

That patience is also where it loses some of its grip. The buildup doesn’t always hold attention equally well, and there are stretches where the story could use more urgency. Being interested in where something is going and being completely absorbed in the journey aren’t quite the same thing.

Still, Crime 101 leaves a solid impression. It’s interesting, entertaining, and a welcome change of pace, even if it doesn’t keep that energy consistent all the way through. Sometimes a good crime flick is enough to make a night at the movies worthwhile.
