“Same family chaos. Still a good time.”

Focker In-Law sticks to the family-comedy formula we enjoy about this franchise. It does not try to change everything or turn the series into something else, and that familiarity works in its favor. There is something satisfying about another round of the same awkward family chaos when you are already having a good time with these characters.

It is a comfortable, enjoyable continuation: familiar jokes, familiar rhythms, and another reason to keep following the Fockers.
