“When watching becomes part of the nightmare”

Faces of Death brings its notorious title into the age of content moderation and endless scrolling. A social-media moderator comes across disturbing videos that resemble the old cult film, turning the question of what’s real into something much harder to keep at a distance.

There’s an uncomfortable idea built into that setup: watching can feel passive, but somebody is always creating, selecting, sharing, or profiting from what ends up on a screen. Horror has plenty to work with in the space between curiosity and the urge to look away.

It makes for a good watch for us. The premise gives the disturbing material something to circle around, and the overall pull is strong enough to carry the experience. A decent horror idea with a nastier attraction underneath it.
