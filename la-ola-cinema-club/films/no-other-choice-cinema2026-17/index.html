No Other Choice

ComedyThriller

2025 · Directed by Park Chan-wook · Comedy · Thriller

The Club
8 /10
The Brain

The Heart
4 /5
“He had other choices. That’s what makes it hard to swallow”

Providing for your family sounds like a reason almost anyone can understand. No Other Choice takes that instinct somewhere deeply uncomfortable, making you root for a man while questioning how much you’re willing to excuse just because you understand what he wants.

It’s funny, absorbing, and full of interesting characters. The situations keep unfolding in ways that make it easy to follow someone who is clearly getting in over his head. Even the length feels earned. There wasn’t a stretch that seemed disposable, and the story flows well enough that the subtitles become part of the experience rather than an obstacle.

A little more time inside some of the other characters’ lives would have been welcome. There’s plenty happening around this man’s pursuit, and those glimpses are interesting enough to leave you curious. Still, the movie keeps its focus and holds your attention.

The uncomfortable part is how familiar his sense of desperation can feel. Sometimes wanting something badly enough makes every alternative seem impossible. You stop asking whether the goal is worth it and start treating anything in the way as something to overcome. But if getting there means making everyone around you suffer, maybe what needs to change is the thing you’re chasing.

That’s the bitter pill. The movie makes his pursuit entertaining without making it easy to feel good about. You can understand the pressure, enjoy the humor, and still find yourself thinking: hold on, what the fuck am I rooting for?

A really good film that keeps you engaged in the moment and leaves the harder questions waiting afterward.
