Explosions can’t always hide a hollow, half-baked story.

Afterburn slides into the 2025 action lineup as one of those mid-tier entries that reminds you how saturated the genre is. It doesn’t try to reinvent the wheel, but it does lean on familiar tropes: the lone hero with a dream, a city in flames, and just enough spectacle to get butts in seats. In a time when action flicks compete with superhero fatigue and streaming bloat, a movie like this only matters if it can stick the landing.

The landing here is shaky at best. The story drags, weighed down by a protagonist whose dream feels lame and undercooked, and pacing that often slips into boring. It’s saved from being a complete wash by a handful of strong action sequences — moments where the choreography and chaos remind you why you showed up in the first place. Still, it’s hard not to walk away thinking this was more filler than fire.
