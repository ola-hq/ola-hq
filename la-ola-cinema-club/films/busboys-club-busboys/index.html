“A career upgrade is a pretty questionable life plan”

Busboys starts with a beautifully dumb piece of logic: two friends decide that becoming waiters will fix their lives. David Spade and Theo Von bring that plan into a restaurant world where moving up one rung apparently means everything else will fall into place.

That’s a perfectly usable foundation for a buddy comedy. Small ambitions, questionable decisions and two people convinced they’ve found the shortcut can be enough. The whole thing depends on whether spending time with them becomes more rewarding than the plan itself.

For us, the result sits below the promise of the setup. There’s a clear lane for this kind of silly, low-stakes escape, but this one doesn’t turn that invitation into much enthusiasm. An easy premise to understand, a harder movie to recommend.
