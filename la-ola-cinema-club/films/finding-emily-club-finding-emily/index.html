“Looking for the right person can send you the wrong way”

Finding Emily starts with a wonderfully inconvenient romantic problem: meeting somebody who seems perfect, then discovering you've been given the wrong phone number. A lovesick musician teams up with a psychology student to track down the girl he can't stop thinking about.

The setup plays with the gap between wanting a person and wanting the story you've already built around them. A search like this can become its own distraction, especially when the person helping you is right there sharing the experience.

It doesn't need to reinvent the romantic comedy to make the journey enjoyable. The familiar pieces leave room for warmth, awkwardness, and the fun of watching a plan become more complicated than expected. A charming, easy watch with enough personality to make the familiar setup feel worthwhile.
