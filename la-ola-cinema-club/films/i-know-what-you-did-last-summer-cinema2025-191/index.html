Another body count, another reboot — same tired shadows.

This franchise has been slashed and revived more times than most horror icons. The latest reboot tries to thread the needle between Gen-Z slasher tropes and nostalgic callbacks, hoping to keep the hook sharp for a new audience. But horror today has a higher bar — audiences want clever reinventions, not just recycled kills with a modern filter.

The movie plays it safe while pretending it doesn’t. Yes, there are some wild twists, but most land in the what the fuck category rather than the clever one. The ending reveal feels forced, the callbacks to the originals are serviceable but hollow, and the “gotcha” moments stretch believability past the breaking point. On the upside, the new cast holds their own — the teen leads have charisma, and the acting isn’t the problem here. But unless you’re already invested in this franchise, there’s nothing new pulling you in. Cameos and callbacks might give fans a jolt, but it’s not worth losing sleep over.

From the Brain

Shallow reinvention, hollow callbacks, nothing we haven’t seen before.

From the Heart
