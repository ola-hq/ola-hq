“A strange, scary loop that feels like watching a video game”

Return to Silent Hill feels like watching a video game unfold. It’s fun, scary, and intriguing, with enough going on beneath the surface to leave you thinking about how it all connects.

That game-like feeling defines the experience. It gives the movie a distinct appeal, even if the result lands somewhere between loving it and hating it. There’s something interesting here without it becoming a film you fully connect with.

The ending is what gives it more weight. The way it circles back around makes the story feel deeper, bringing you back to the intensity of that love and everything wrapped up in it. It’s the part that leaves you turning the movie over afterward.

A good flick with an intriguing finish—enjoyable enough, even if it never quite becomes a favorite.
