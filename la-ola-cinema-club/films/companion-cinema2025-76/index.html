Twists on twists — the Black Mirror episode that grew into a full-blown movie.

This one lives in that slick Black Mirror lane — a near-future thriller that feels close enough to our reality to be scary. It takes on the “can we outsmart AI/tech” question in a way that’s both unsettling and entertaining, giving you a peek at a possible tomorrow that’s just around the corner.

From start to finish it’s a thrill ride — full of curveballs, fake-outs, and reveals that keep you second-guessing. Some you see coming, some smack you in the head out of nowhere. That unpredictability is half the fun. Excellent acting all around and a story that balances sci-fi with grounded “what if” tension. If you’re into tech, thrillers, or just want a cheeky mystery with futuristic vibes, this one hits.
