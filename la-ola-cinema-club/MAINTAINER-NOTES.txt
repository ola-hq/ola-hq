LA OLA CINEMA CLUB

This release reconciles44 existing placeholders and adds44 films, preserving the190 original film IDs and125 archive records.

Data lives in data/films.json, data/archives.json and data/guide.json. Every film has a stableID and canonical films/<slug>/ path. Combined clubScore is outof10; legacy score is retained outof5 for the HQ reader. Do not change numeric ratings when changing display formatting.

Guide shelves show high-scoring examples rather than an asserted personal favorites list. Current full captions and card highlights have separate reviewKind values. Concept films are kept outside the review dataset.

Saved favorites are device-local. Historical Blockbuster URLs forward to the new routes while preserving query strings and film hashes.

Run node --test scripts/cinema.test.mjs and node scripts/verify-hq-release.mjs from the repository root before publication. Canonical editorial source and publication manifest live in the private Cinema project.
