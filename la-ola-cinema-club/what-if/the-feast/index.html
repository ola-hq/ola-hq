What If? / An imagined film

The Feast

What if the first Thanksgiving were a horror movie?

Imagine a group of European settlers escaping poverty, persecution, and a life where powerful people control everything.

After a brutal voyage, they arrive in a beautiful new world. Indigenous communities welcome them, help them survive, and introduce them to a way of life completely different from their own.

At first, it’s almost magical.

The families share food. Their children grow up together. Friendships form. Cultures intertwine.

But slowly, something begins changing.

A child is mistreated. Someone disappears. A relationship turns into a manipulation. A disagreement becomes an excuse for violence.

Every incident seems isolated. Every explanation sounds reasonable.

And somehow, the newcomers keep benefiting.

All the while, both communities are preparing for an extraordinary harvest celebration.

A feast.

Everyone gathers. Music plays. Food is shared. People give thanks.

It’s beautiful.

Reveal the turn

Except the audience has begun noticing things the characters haven’t.

Certain families are missing. Certain smiles feel wrong. Certain conversations suddenly sound threatening.

And as the celebration unfolds, we discover just how much has been happening behind the scenes.

The people we spent the first half of the movie rooting for may be the very people we should have feared.

A psychological horror concept about survival becoming entitlement, hospitality becoming vulnerability, and the terrifying ability of ordinary people to justify cruelty.

An original imagined film concept, shared for discussion and creative inspiration. Inspired by the history of European colonization; not a factual reconstruction of the 1621 Thanksgiving. Concept illustration, not artwork from a released film.
