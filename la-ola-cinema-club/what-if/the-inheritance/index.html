What If? / An imagined film

The Inheritance

What if the inheritance was the horror?

A settler escapes oppression and builds a new life through an Indigenous community’s generosity.

He registers its land in his name.

Decades later, his son discovers that the inheritance grants him the power to take everything from the people who welcomed his father.

They inherited the land. Not the promise.

The question at the center

What happens when the next generation inherits power over people whose hospitality made that power possible?

This version follows the slower, more disturbing consequences of inherited power. Where The Feast is immediate and paranoid, The Inheritance lets the horror unfold across generations.

An original imagined film concept, shared for discussion and creative inspiration. Inspired by the history of European colonization; not a factual reconstruction of the 1621 Thanksgiving. Generated concept artwork, not images from a released film.
