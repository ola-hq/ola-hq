About usage tracking

We count visits, pages, link and button clicks, and supported app installation events to understand how people use OLA HQ and La Ola de Amor. Reports are private to the hosts.

A random identifier saved in your browser helps count repeat visits. We do not record names, guestbook messages, passwords, form values, or URL query strings in these analytics. Referrers contain only the website’s hostname. The collector briefly uses an IP address to create a daily keyed digest for rate limiting; raw IP addresses are not saved in the analytics table. Hosting providers may keep their own operational logs.

Visitors are browser estimates, not an exact count of people. Private browsing, multiple devices, privacy settings, and unsupported install events affect the totals. We respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control.
